What's included

The full core CRM, plus what travel & visa teams use.

Enquiries

Walk-in, WhatsApp, Facebook and website enquiries on one board, from new to converted.

Visa cases

Numbered cases moving from documents to submission and decision, with fee, appointment and outcome.

Document checklists

Each visa type's checklist copied onto every case, with uploads, verification and expiry dates.

Payments & receipts

Payments and refunds with automatic receipt numbers, balances updated on the case, mistakes voided not deleted.

Reminders

Office overview
