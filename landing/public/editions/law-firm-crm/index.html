What's included

The full core CRM, plus what legal & law teams use.

Client intake

Enquiries move from new to consultation, proposal and retained. A retained enquiry opens the matter and makes the contact a client.

Matters & conflict checks

Numbered matters with practice area, lead lawyer, opposing party and court. Every matter is checked for possible conflicts automatically.

Hearings & deadlines

Hearings, filing deadlines and limitation dates on each matter, with the next key date always on show.

Time recording

Hours at the matter's rate, with each matter's unbilled work always up to date.

Invoices & payments

Numbered invoices that pick up unbilled time and fixed fees, payments with receipt numbers, balances kept current.

Practice overview

Open matters, unbilled work, outstanding invoices, hours by lawyer and enquiries by source on one dashboard.
