What's included

The full core CRM, plus what call centre teams use.

Campaigns

Outbound sales, collections, surveys or inbound support, each with its script, dates, attempt limits and live totals.

Call lists

Every lead in a campaign with its agent, priority, attempts and next call time, so the queue sorts itself.

Call logging & dispositions

Sale, interested, callback, no answer, busy, voicemail, wrong number or do-not-call, with notes, duration and recording link.

Callbacks

Pick a time and the callback lands in the agent's tasks and back at the top of the list when it's due.

Do-not-call

One do-not-call disposition flags the person and removes them from every open campaign.

Floor dashboard

Calls by outcome, agent and campaign, contact and conversion counts, and what's due now.

Automations

Follow-up that happens on its own.

Call Centre CRM questions

A call centre CRM organises who to call, records what happened on every call and decides the next step automatically: retries, callbacks, conversions and do-not-call. SalesKnot does this on top of a full sales CRM, so converted leads flow straight into your pipeline.

Yes. Agents can log calls by hand, or your dialer (such as Twilio, Aircall or 3CX) can send finished calls to SalesKnot automatically through the API, with the recording link attached.

Each campaign has an attempt limit and a retry wait. No-answer calls come back after the wait until the limit is reached; callbacks are booked for the exact time the customer asked for.
