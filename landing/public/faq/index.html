FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything teams ask us about SalesKnot: pricing, features, data, security and getting started.

General

SalesKnot is an automation-first CRM for sales teams. It keeps your leads, contacts, companies and deals in one place, and uses workflows and AI agents to handle routine follow-up automatically. It is built and supported by OpsKnot.

Most CRMs wait for someone to type. SalesKnot is set up so routine work happens on its own: new leads are assigned, follow-ups are scheduled, records are updated and reminders go out, with AI agents handling the repetitive steps.

Small and mid-sized sales teams that want a CRM working for them rather than another tool to update, including call centres, real estate, travel and visa agencies, legal services and import/export. We work with teams in the United States, United Kingdom, UAE and worldwide.
