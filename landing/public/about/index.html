About

We built the CRM we kept wishing for.

SalesKnot is made by OpsKnot, a CRM and automation consultancy. After years of setting up CRMs that teams forgot to update, we built one that updates itself.

Most CRMs are expensive filing cabinets. They charge for every seat, and they only work if salespeople stop selling to type in what happened. So the data goes stale, follow-ups slip, and the CRM becomes something people avoid.

SalesKnot flips that. It's automation-first: workflows and AI agents capture leads, assign them, follow up and keep records tidy, so the CRM works for the team instead of the other way round.

And it's priced the way small and growing teams need: one flat price per company, so hiring another salesperson never means a bigger software bill.

We set every workspace up ourselves, import your data and stay on hand afterwards. You talk to the people who built your system, not a ticket queue.
