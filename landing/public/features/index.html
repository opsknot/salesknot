Workflow automation

Follow-up that runs itself.

Build workflows that react to what happens in your CRM: a new lead, a deal changing stage, a date arriving or a form being submitted. SalesKnot does the next step, every time.

  • Triggers on new or updated records, schedules, forms and webhooks
  • Assign owners, create tasks, update fields and send emails
  • Branch on conditions, like deal size or lead source
  • Call other tools with HTTP requests and custom code steps

AI agents

AI that handles the busywork.

AI agents are steps in your workflows that read a record, think, and act. We set them up around your process, so they do the repetitive work your team would otherwise put off.

  • Qualify and score new leads from what they wrote
  • Summarise long email threads onto the deal
  • Draft personalised follow-ups for review
  • Clean and complete records: names, companies, next steps

  • Amounts, close dates, owners and next steps on every deal
  • Full history of every change and activity

Custom objects & fields

A CRM shaped to your business.

Add your own record types, fields and relations without code. Properties, trips, matters, shipments: whatever your business tracks, SalesKnot can hold it and automate around it.

  • Custom objects and relations
  • Text, number, currency, date, select, rating, links and more
  • Page layouts and views for each object
  • Roles that control who sees and edits what
