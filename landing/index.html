Questions

Common questions

How is SalesKnot priced?

One flat monthly price per company, not per user, so adding salespeople doesn't raise your bill. Setup, data import and custom automations are quoted up front after a short call.

What does automation-first mean?

Most CRMs wait for someone to type. SalesKnot is set up so routine work happens on its own: new leads are assigned, follow-ups are scheduled, records are updated and reminders go out, with AI agents handling the repetitive steps.

Do I need an industry edition?

No. The standard CRM covers companies, people, deals, tasks, notes and workflows. An edition adds the records and dashboards your sector uses, such as properties and showings for real estate.

Is my data kept separate from other customers?

Yes. Each company gets its own workspace and its own address, with its own users and data. Connections are encrypted, and you can export your data at any time.

Can you move us from another CRM or spreadsheets?

Yes. We import your contacts, companies and deals and rebuild the key automations, so your team starts with its history in place.

Who is behind SalesKnot?

SalesKnot is built and supported by OpsKnot, a CRM and automation consultancy. You deal directly with the people who set up your system.
